What it looks like in practice

A typical enquiry-handling process, before and after automation.

Before

  1. An email arrives in a shared inbox
  2. Someone reads it and copies details into a spreadsheet
  3. Someone else updates the CRM
  4. A report is assembled by hand
  5. A notification is sent manually

After

  1. The enquiry is captured automatically
  2. AI extracts and classifies the key details
  3. The CRM and spreadsheet update themselves
  4. A draft reply is prepared for review
  5. The right person is notified instantly
